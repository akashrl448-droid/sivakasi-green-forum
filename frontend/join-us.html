Volunteer FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any previous environmental or planting experience?

None at all. Our team provides an orientation at the start of every volunteer session, along with all tools, gloves, and guidance needed.

How often are field restoration days organized?

We typically host volunteer drives on alternating Saturdays and during special community plantation weekends during pre-monsoon seasons.

Can I donate to a specific project instead of the general fund?

Yes — every donation option is tied to a specific project so you always know exactly what your support is going toward.

Is there an age requirement for volunteering?

Volunteers under 16 are welcome for school and awareness programs; field restoration days require volunteers to be 16 or older.
