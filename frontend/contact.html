Common Questions

Before You Write In

I want to volunteer — should I use this form or the Join Us page?

Use the Join Us Today page for volunteering, membership, and donations — it routes directly to our volunteer coordinator and gets a faster response.

How quickly will I hear back?

General inquiries through this form typically get a reply within 2 working days. Media and partnership requests may take slightly longer.

Can I visit an active project site?

Yes — most ongoing sites welcome visitors during scheduled volunteer days. Mention which project you're interested in and we'll share the next open date.
