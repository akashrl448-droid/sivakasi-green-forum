Our Passion

Bridging the gap to restore our green environment

Within the limits set by nature, we try to bridge the gap, addressing green issues, advancing green principles, restoring green environment in the Industrial City — Sivakasi. We unite businesses, government, students, and social organizations around environmental protection.

Through active participation, public campaign efforts, and physical volunteering, we bring restoration directly to dry water reserves and depleted green covers in our home region.

Our Mission

Paving the way for bio-diversity and health

Ensuring environmental safety & health and protection of natural reservoirs; establishing a green environment and thereby paving way for bio-diversity.
