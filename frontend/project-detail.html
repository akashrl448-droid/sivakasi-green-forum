Delta Floodplain Reconnection

Reconnecting 340 acres of historic floodplain to reduce flood risk downstream and rebuild the native wetland habitat.

Project Overview

For four decades, a disconnected levee cut off 340 acres of natural floodplain from the catchment channel. SGF is reopening this connection in phased breaches to allow seasonal waters to nourish aquifers.

Key Objectives

  • ✓ Reconnect 340 acres of historic floodplain to the channel
  • ✓ Reduce peak monsoon flood levels for 3 downstream villages
  • ✓ Re-establish native wetland vegetation and nursery grounds
